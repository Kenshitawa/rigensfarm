Data Protection

Privacy Policy & Data Stewardship.

RigenFarms is committed to safeguarding the privacy and personal data of our donors, volunteers, school partners, and community members in accordance with Kenyan law.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum necessary information required to facilitate our charitable operations, school garden installations, and donor stewardship:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, telephone/WhatsApp number, and location when you submit an inquiry, volunteer application, or newsletter subscription.
  • Contribution Details: Transaction confirmation codes, donation amounts, and dates (we never store banking credentials or credit card numbers directly).
  • Volunteer Profiles: Skills, availability preferences, and county of residence to pair applicants with local agricultural projects.

2. How We Use Information

Your data is used strictly for legitimate charitable purposes:

  • Communicating field progress, project milestones, and impact reports.
  • Issuing official donation acknowledgments and financial receipts.
  • Coordinating volunteer field visits and safety orientations.
  • Responding to partnership inquiries from headteachers and local organizations.

3. Child & Student Safeguarding

RigenFarms works extensively in primary and secondary schools across Kenya. We take pupil protection with the highest gravity:

All photography and testimonials featuring minor students are obtained with prior institutional consent from school headmasters, teachers, and parent associations. We do not publish minor students' full legal names or private addresses under any circumstances.

4. Security & Data Retention

We implement appropriate technical and administrative safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure of your personal data. We retain records only as long as necessary to comply with Kenyan legal, auditing, and non-profit regulatory requirements.

5. Third-Party Sharing & Non-Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information to third parties. Information is only shared with authorized partners (such as mobile money providers or banks) solely to process contributions or when required by statutory Kenyan law.

6. Data Rights & Contact

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, you have the right to request access to the information we hold about you, request corrections, or ask for your contact details to be deleted from our mailing lists.

For any privacy inquiries or data requests, contact our desk at rigenfarms@gmail.com or phone +254 706 476 939.
