Join The Movement

Get your hands in the soil & grow change.

Whether you are an agronomist, student, educator, or creative storyteller, your energy helps us transform school compounds and community food security across Kenya.

Opportunities

Volunteer Pathways

Select the track where your passion and skills make the deepest community impact.

Hands-On Garden Builder

Work on the ground in Kisumu and Siaya constructing wicking bed containers, mixing living biochar soil, and laying out school garden plots.

  • Wicking bed carpentry & plumbing
  • Soil preparation & transplanting
  • Commitment: 4–8 hrs/week (Flexible)
Apply for this Track

School Garden Mentor

Facilitate hands-on science and biology sessions with primary school learners, teaching them wicking water cycles and organic composting.

  • Student club facilitation
  • Living lab lesson support
  • Commitment: 1–2 school visits/week
Apply for this Track

Agronomy & Soil Specialist

Provide technical guidance on natural pest prevention, bio-fertilizer teas, seed saving, and yield optimization across our 500+ container network.

  • Organic pest control protocols
  • Seedling nursery propagation
  • Commitment: Flexible / Project-based
Apply for this Track

Media & Community Storyteller

Capture authentic photography, record farmer testimonials, and create engaging social updates that amplify grassroots climate resilience.

  • Field photography & video capture
  • Social media & newsletter writing
  • Commitment: Remote or Field
Apply for this Track

Youth Agripreneur Coach

Coach young rural entrepreneurs on bookkeeping, local produce marketing, customer management, and micro-business viability.

  • Business plan mentorship
  • Market linkage connections
  • Commitment: 2–4 hrs/month
Apply for this Track

Custom Contribution

Have a unique skill not listed here? (e.g. web engineering, grant writing, legal advisory, logistical support). We would love to plug you in.

  • Specialized professional advisory
  • Strategic institutional linkages
  • Tailored to your availability
