Why partner with us

From training to transformation.

We build lasting capacity — not dependency. Every RigenFarms program is designed so communities can sustain, teach, and expand it themselves.

Proven Wicking Technology

Up to 50% water saving. Works in any climate. Built from locally available materials that any community can source and maintain independently.

Community-Led Design

We co-design every program with the communities we serve. Local ownership means programs outlast our involvement — and spread further.

Open & Transparent

Annual reports, open data, honest impact measurement. We publish what we learn — including what didn't work — so others can build on it.

Multi-sector Partnerships

Working alongside county governments, NGOs, universities, and schools to scale climate-smart agriculture across 6 Kenyan counties and beyond.

How we work

From community to harvest.

Every RigenFarms program follows a proven four-step process that puts community knowledge and ownership at the centre.

1

Community Assessment

We visit and listen first — mapping water access, soil type, food challenges, and local knowledge before designing anything.

2

Training & Co-design

Hands-on workshops with farmers, teachers, and youth to build, understand, and adapt wicking garden systems together.

3

Installation & Planting

Gardens go in together — school beds, community hubs, or home kitchen gardens — with starter seeds and technical support.

4

Monitor & Scale

We track yields, water savings, and nutrition outcomes — then help communities teach the method to their neighbours and schools.
