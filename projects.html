Flagship Program

School Food & Nutrition Gardens

We partner directly with primary and secondary schools to install closed-loop wicking container garden systems. These beds serve as outdoor living laboratories where learners practice climate-smart biology while cultivating fresh spinach, kale, and indigenous vegetables for daily school lunch supplementation.

25 Partner Schools 5,000+ Meals Boosted
Water Innovation

Sub-Surface Wicking Bed Technology

Our flagship water-conservation system uses capillary action to water crops from the bottom up. By keeping moisture enclosed beneath a protective barrier, the wicking container slashes irrigation water consumption by up to 50%, prevents root evaporation, and resists drought cycles effortlessly.

Bridging the gap between rural youth unemployment and modern high-value agriculture. We train out-of-school youth in container horticulture, nursery management, organic compost production, and digital market linkage so they can operate profitable micro-farms in their own communities.

Engineering Breakdown

How wicking container technology works

A low-cost, closed-loop sub-surface capillary watering system engineered from recycled materials to eliminate waste and guarantee plant vigor.

01

Enclosed Reservoir

A waterproof containment basin at the bottom stores water and dissolved organic nutrients safely away from sun exposure, stopping surface evaporation completely.

02

Geotextile Divider

A porous separator prevents soil particles from collapsing into the reservoir while allowing water vapor and moisture to migrate upward freely.

03

Capillary Wicking Action

As the root zone absorbs water, physics naturally draws moisture upward through capillary tension in the soil matrix. Plants drink on demand without water stress.

04

Built-In Overflow Valve

An engineered drainage point ensures the soil never becomes waterlogged or anaerobic during heavy monsoon downpours, preventing root rot automatically.
