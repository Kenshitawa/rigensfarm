Scientific Rigor

How we measure outcomes

We combine baseline socio-economic surveys with continuous agronomic tracking before, during, and after each growing cycle.

  • Pre- and Post-Training Knowledge Audits: Quantifying farmer retention of composting, pest control, and water balance.
  • Water Metering & Evaporation Comparison: Side-by-side logs proving up to 50% water savings over open furrow irrigation.
  • School Meal Supplementation Logs: Daily kilogram records of greens integrated into pupil dining halls.
  • Youth Enterprise Revenue Tracking: Auditing smallholder profits, reinvestment, and household food savings.
