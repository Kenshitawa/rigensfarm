Origin & Purpose

Cultivating climate resilience from the ground up.

Founded in Kisumu in 2022, RigenFarms transforms semi-arid and vulnerable school compounds into self-sustaining food havens using climate-smart wicking garden containers.

Our Story

From one school garden to a regional movement.

In 2022, western Kenya experienced unpredictable drought cycles that devastated local harvests and disrupted school lunch programs. Observing how students struggled to focus on empty stomachs, our founders set out to design a food production model that didn't rely on expensive irrigation systems or huge acreage.

We developed accessible wicking container systems from recycled materials and locally sourced volcanic aggregates. These self-watering beds consume up to 50% less water while generating bumper yields of nutrient-dense indigenous greens and vegetables right on school grounds.

Vision & Mission

Vision: A Kenya where every school and community has autonomous food security, sustainable water stewardship, and climate resilience driven by young growers.

Mission: To equip Kenyan schools, youth agripreneurs, and smallholder farmers with practical wicking-bed technology, regenerative soil training, and continuous field mentorship.

Our North Star

The principles that guide our work

Every seed planted, wicking container engineered, and workshop hosted is grounded in six uncompromising commitments.

Water Stewardship

Water is life. We optimize every drop through sub-surface capillary wicking systems that minimize evaporative loss and recycle rainwater runoff.

Community-Led

We never impose external blueprints. Solutions are co-designed with teachers, headmasters, village elders, and youth farmer groups.

Regenerative Ecology

Zero chemical runoff. We enrich living soils with biochar, compost teas, natural pest deterrents, and indigenous crop varieties.

Youth Empowerment

Transforming agriculture from an onerous chore into an exciting, tech-enabled enterprise that dignifies and inspires the next generation.

Radical Integrity

Transparent financial reporting, open-source educational manuals, and faithful accountability to donors, volunteers, and communities.

Frugal Innovation

Building resilient agricultural systems from locally accessible, affordable, and durable recycled materials that anyone can maintain.

Milestones

Our journey of growth & impact

From small-scale trials in Kisumu to institutional recognition across 6 Kenyan counties.

2022

Founding & First School Prototype

RigenFarms is registered in Kisumu, Kenya. We build our first pilot wicking bed at a local primary school, demonstrating 50% water savings and yielding fresh spinach for over 200 pupil meals weekly.

2023

Scaling to 10 Schools & Community Nurseries

Expanded across Kisumu and Siaya counties. Established our central tree and vegetable seedling nursery, distributing over 4,000 seedlings and training 600+ youth agripreneurs.

2024

6 Counties, 25 Schools & University Collaborations

Formalized academic partnerships with Maseno University and Great Lakes University of Kisumu. Reached 2,000+ community members trained and crossed 500 wicking container installations.

2025 & Beyond

Regional Agro-Hub & Circular Innovation

Expanding climate training hubs, introducing biochar enrichment systems, and scaling school nutrition programs across Western and Rift Valley Kenya.
